## 1. Overview
Tony G. Studios ("we," "our," or "us") built LingoLounge as a global real-time chat application. This Privacy Policy explains how LingoLounge collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our Android application.

## 2. Information We Collect and Use
To provide real-time messaging, account access, and moderation safety, LingoLounge processes the following data:
- **Account Information:** When you sign in using Email/Password or Google Sign-In, we collect your email address, display name, unique user ID (UID), and preferred language setting.
- **User-Generated Content (Chat Messages & Profile):** Messages you send in public chat rooms, your optional bio or interest tags, and any user safety reports you submit are stored to deliver chat functionality and enforce community rules.
- **Device & Diagnostic Data:** Standard technical identifiers (such as app version, device type, and crash logs) may be processed automatically to keep the service stable and secure.

## 3. On-Device Translation (Google ML Kit)
LingoLounge uses Google ML Kit On-Device Translation to translate chat messages between languages (such as English and Korean). Translation models are downloaded to your Android device and message translations are processed locally on your device rather than sent to an external translation server.

## 4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing
We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with trusted infrastructure providers strictly necessary to operate the app:
- **Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore):** Used to authenticate accounts, store user profiles, sync real-time chat messages, and record moderation reports.

## 5. Data Security
All data transmitted between LingoLounge and our backend servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Access to user records and chat rooms is restricted through Firebase Security Rules.

## 6. Data Retention and Account Deletion
We retain your account profile and messages for as long as your account remains active. You can permanently delete your account and associated personal data at any time by:
1. Opening **Settings** inside LingoLounge and selecting **Delete Account & Data**, or
2. Emailing **antoniogreen267@gmail.com** from your registered email address with the subject line "Account Deletion Request."

Deletion requests are processed promptly, and personal account data is removed from active systems within 30 days.

## 7. Children's Privacy
LingoLounge is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy as new features are added. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
